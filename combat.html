Combat Guide

Master realistic medieval combat. Timing, combos, and tactics.

⚔️ Offense

  • • Master Strike - Perfect timing counters
  • • Combo Chains - String attacks together
  • • Feints - Fake and trick enemies
  • • Weapon Weight - Heavier = more damage, slower

🛡️ Defense

  • • Block - Raise weapon to block
  • • Perfect Block - Timing matters
  • • Dodge - Side-step attacks
  • • Clash - Clashing weapons for openings
